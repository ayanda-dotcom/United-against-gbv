GET HELP

Support should be close when you need it.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services where available. This platform is not an emergency service.

🆘 Find local support

Search for verified helplines, shelters, legal aid and survivor-support organizations by country.

🔐 Protect your privacy

Use a safer device if possible, avoid sharing identifying information publicly, and close this page if doing so feels unsafe.

SAFETY BY DESIGN

Trust is part of the product.

Future versions should use verification, moderation, secure messaging, minimal data collection, consent controls and strong protections against harassment and doxxing.

🛡️

Verified profiles

Separate verified organizations from unverified community accounts and make verification status visible.

👁️

Privacy controls

Give users control over what appears publicly and avoid exposing sensitive personal details.

⚖️

Clear moderation

Provide reporting, blocking, escalation and transparent community rules.
